Why Every Small Business Needs a Professional Website in 2026

G
GetMyWebsite Team
Updated August 2026

In today's digital world, having a professional website is no longer optional — it is essential. Customers search online before buying products, booking services, or contacting a business. If your business doesn't have a website, you're likely losing potential customers to competitors who do.

Your website is your digital storefront

A website is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Even when your office or shop is closed, customers can learn about your services, browse your products, and contact you. This means your business is always available, helping you generate leads around the clock.

Build trust and credibility

People trust businesses with professional websites. A modern website that includes your company information, services, customer reviews, and contact details creates confidence. It shows customers that your business is genuine and committed to quality.

Reach more customers

Unlike a physical shop that serves only local visitors, a website allows people from different cities or even countries to find your business. With proper SEO, your website can appear in Google search results when customers are looking for services you offer.

Increase sales and leads

A well-designed website encourages visitors to take action. Features such as contact forms, WhatsApp integration, online booking, and clear call-to-action buttons make it easy for customers to connect with your business. This increases enquiries and improves sales opportunities.

Improve your marketing results

Whether you advertise on Google, Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn, a website gives your visitors a professional destination. Instead of sending people to a social media profile, you can direct them to a dedicated page that explains your services and encourages conversions.

Showcase your work

A website is the perfect place to display your portfolio, customer testimonials, case studies, and success stories. This helps potential clients understand the quality of your work and makes it easier for them to choose your business.

Gain a competitive advantage

Many small businesses still rely only on social media. By having a fast, responsive, and professional website, you stand out from competitors and create a stronger brand image.

Local SEO Post 05

How to Get Your Business Found on Google Maps

G
GetMyWebsite Team
Updated August 2026

When someone nearby searches for "restaurant near me" or "dentist near me," Google shows a map with a handful of businesses first — before any regular search results. This is called the Local Pack, and getting into it can bring in more walk-in customers than almost anything else you do online. Here's what actually moves the needle.

Claim and verify your Google Business Profile

This is the free listing that shows your name, address, hours, photos and reviews on Google Maps and in search. If you haven't claimed it yet, this is the single highest-impact thing you can do this week. Verification usually happens by postcard, phone call, or email, depending on your business type.

Keep your details consistent everywhere

Google checks your name, address and phone number against directories, your website, and social pages. If they don't match exactly — even a slightly different spelling of your street — it can quietly hurt your ranking. Keep this information identical across your website, Google profile, and every directory you're listed on.

Add real photos regularly

Listings with recent, genuine photos of the shop front, food, or work outperform listings with none. Skip stock photos — Google and customers can both tell.

Collect reviews, and reply to them

Review count and rating are direct ranking factors for the Local Pack. Just as important: replying to reviews, good or bad, signals that a business is active and attentive.

Link your website to your profile

Your Google Business Profile should link straight to your website, and your website should reflect the exact same name, address and phone number. This two-way consistency is part of what earns trust with Google's local algorithm.

We set up and optimise Google Business Profiles as part of our digital marketing service, alongside a website built to match — so your online presence tells one consistent story.

How Much Should a Small Business Website Actually Cost?

G
GetMyWebsite Team
Updated August 2026

This is one of the most common questions we get, and the honest answer is: it depends on what you actually need, not on a fixed price list. Here's how to think about it so you don't overpay or underbuy.

What drives the price up or down

Number of pages, custom design versus a template, features like online booking or ordering, whether you need copywriting done for you, and how fast you need it delivered — these are the real variables. A simple one-page site for a cafe costs far less than a multi-page site with online ordering for a restaurant chain.

What a fair small-business website usually includes

  • A custom design, not a generic drag-and-drop template
  • Mobile-first layout, since most visitors arrive on a phone
  • Contact details, WhatsApp click-to-chat, and a map
  • Basic SEO setup so the site is at least crawlable and indexable
  • Hosting guidance so you're not stuck once it's delivered

Red flags to watch for

Extremely cheap "unlimited page" offers are usually template sites with little customisation. On the other end, agencies quoting a large monthly retainer for a basic brochure site are often overcharging for what small businesses actually need.

Why we start with a free mockup

Instead of quoting blind, we build a free mockup first so you can see exactly what you'd be getting before agreeing to anything. It removes the guesswork on both sides.

Why Every Local Business Should Add WhatsApp to Their Website

G
GetMyWebsite Team
Updated August 2026

In India especially, most customers would rather message a business on WhatsApp than fill out a contact form or make a phone call. A website without a WhatsApp button is quietly losing enquiries it could have kept.

It matches how customers already behave

People already have WhatsApp open. A click-to-chat button removes every bit of friction between "interested" and "actually messaging you" — no dialling, no waiting on hold, no filling in a form and waiting for a reply.

It works for every kind of enquiry

Booking a table, asking if an item is in stock, requesting a quote, confirming an appointment — WhatsApp handles all of it in one thread, with photos and voice notes if needed, which a contact form simply can't do.

It builds trust faster

A quick, personal WhatsApp reply feels more human than an automated "we'll get back to you" email. For service businesses especially — salons, clinics, contractors — that immediacy often decides whether someone books with you or moves to the next search result.

How to set it up properly

A simple click-to-chat link (using your number in the URL) works instantly with no setup. For businesses that get a high volume of messages, WhatsApp Business adds catalogues, automated replies, and quick-reply templates on top of the same number.

Every website we build includes a WhatsApp click-to-chat button by default — because for most small businesses, it converts better than a traditional contact form alone.

Why Restaurants and Cloud Kitchens Need a Website, Not Just Zomato

G
GetMyWebsite Team
Updated August 2026

Many restaurant and cloud kitchen owners assume Zomato or Swiggy is enough. It brings orders, sure — but it also comes with commissions on every sale, no control over how your brand looks, and zero ownership of your own customer relationships. A website fixes all three.

You keep more of every order

Delivery platforms typically take a meaningful commission per order. A website with your own ordering flow, or even just a WhatsApp-based ordering system, lets you keep repeat customers ordering directly instead of paying a cut every single time.

You control the brand experience

On Zomato, every restaurant looks the same — same layout, same fonts, competitors' ads next to your menu. Your own website is entirely yours: your photography, your story, your offers, with nothing pulling attention away.

You own the customer relationship

Delivery apps own the customer data, not you. A website with a simple sign-up or WhatsApp opt-in means you can actually reach past customers directly with offers, instead of relying entirely on the platform's algorithm to resurface you.

It supports your reviews and reputation

A website with real photos, an actual menu, and genuine reviews makes a much stronger first impression than a delivery-app listing squeezed between five competitors. It's also where you can point customers who found you on Instagram or Google, rather than losing them mid-search on someone else's app.

We build QR menus, ordering flows and review-boosting tools specifically for restaurants and cloud kitchens — designed to work alongside Zomato and Swiggy, not depend entirely on them.
